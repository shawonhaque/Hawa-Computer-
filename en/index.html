Are you a government office?+

No. We are an independent service assistance centre. Applications are submitted on official portals and approval is decided by the relevant authority.

How do I pay?+

bKash, Nagad, Rocket, card, bank or cash at the shop. For online payments, submit the transaction ID and our team verifies it.

How long does an order take?+

Each service page shows the estimated time. Contact us for urgent requests.

Are my documents safe?+

Uploaded files are stored privately and only authorised staff can access them.

Will my photos be public?+

No. Customer photographs are never shown publicly.

What is the refund policy?+

Full refund before work starts, partial once work has begun. See the refund policy for details.
